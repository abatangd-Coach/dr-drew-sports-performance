The Main Focus

Youth athletes need a foundation, not random conditioning.

Many young athletes are told to “get faster,” “get stronger,” or “work harder,” but they are rarely given a structured plan that matches their age, sport, training history, movement quality, and current capacity.

Dr. Drew Sports Performance helps youth athletes develop the qualities that matter most: movement skill, strength, coordination, speed, durability, confidence, and consistency.

Running Performance

Build durability and reduce breakdown.

Strength and movement programming for youth runners, recreational runners, and athletes returning to running.

  • Running-specific strength

  • Training load guidance

Golf Performance

Move better and create more efficient power.

Performance coaching for golfers who need better mobility, strength, rotational power, and durability.

  • Hip and thoracic mobility
  • Rotational strength and power
  • Core control and sequencing
  • Low back, hip, and shoulder resilience
  • Tournament preparation and recovery
Return to Sport

Bridge the gap between rehab and performance.

For athletes who are cleared from rehab but do not yet feel ready, strong, explosive, or confident.

  • Strength progressions
  • Jumping, landing, and cutting progressions
  • Return-to-running plans
  • Sport-specific reloading
  • Milestone tracking
BMX Performance

Explosive starts, sprint power, and race readiness.

BMX remains a specialty area, with programming for riders who want better gate starts, sprint power, mobility, and recovery.

  • Gate start development
  • Sprint and acceleration training
  • Strength and power programming
  • Race-week preparation
  • Mobility and recovery routines
